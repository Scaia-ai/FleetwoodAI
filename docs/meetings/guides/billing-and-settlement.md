## Questions

1. What makes an order billable?
2. What prevents an order from being invoiced?
3. How often are invoices generated, and by whom?
4. Are invoices generated by customer, date range, batch, billing sequence, or another grouping?
5. What invoice formats are sent to customers?
6. How are payments applied to invoices?
7. How are credits created and approved?
8. What is the difference between no-charge, credit, adjustment, and cancellation?
9. How are COD amounts handled from order to settlement/accounting?
10. How are fuel charges calculated and changed?
11. How are discounts applied?
12. How are vendor settlements calculated?
13. How do employee settlements differ from vendor settlements?
14. How are sales commissions calculated?
15. What causes recalculation today?
16. Which financial values are allowed to be manually overridden?
17. What audit trail is required for financial changes?
18. What reports are required for month-end close?
19. Which current billing steps are slow, risky, or manual?

## Workflow To Validate

1. Completed orders are reviewed.
2. Charges are confirmed or corrected.
3. Invoice batch is generated.
4. Invoice is printed, emailed, exported, or otherwise delivered.
5. Customer payments and credits are applied.
6. Vendor/employee settlement is generated.
7. Commission and management reports are reviewed.
8. Reconciliation or recalculation is performed if needed.

- Which billing exceptions require human judgment?
- Would natural-language explanations of invoice totals help internal users or customers?
